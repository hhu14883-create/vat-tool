Standard Rate

Applied to most goods and services. Used as the default rate for B2C sales in the destination country.

Reduced Rates

Lower rates for essentials like food, books, medicine, and children's items. Varies by member state.

OSS & IOSS

EU sellers may use One-Stop Shop (OSS). Non-EU sellers often register for Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS) for parcels under EUR 150.

Rates are for reference only. VAT rules change frequently - always verify with your tax advisor or local tax authority before filing.
