What is VAT?

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax collected at each stage of the supply chain. Businesses registered for VAT charge VAT on sales (output VAT), reclaim VAT on business purchases (input VAT), and pay the net difference to the tax authority.

For e-commerce, the practical question is usually: which country's VAT applies, who must register, and which rate band applies to this SKU? The EU sets framework rules; each country sets rates and most compliance details.

How to calculate VAT-inclusive prices

Use the same base your storefront uses (gross vs net pricing).

Add VAT to a net price (ex VAT):

Gross = Net × (1 + VAT% / 100)

Example: EUR 100 net at 21% → 100 × 1.21 = EUR 121 gross.

Extract VAT from a gross price (VAT included):

Net = Gross / (1 + VAT% / 100)

VAT amount = Gross − Net

Example: EUR 121 gross at 21% → net 121 / 1.21 = 100, VAT EUR 21.

Marketplaces may display VAT differently (VAT inclusive checkout vs business invoices). Always align your product feed, invoices, and OSS return to the same logic.

Seller FAQ

What is the difference between OSS and IOSS?

Union OSS lets you report and pay VAT on eligible B2C sales of goods and services to consumers across the EU from one registration. IOSS is only for import VAT on low-value consignments (not exceeding EUR 150) when sold B2C from outside the EU and declared under the scheme.

Which VAT rate do I show at checkout?

For B2C where your supply is taxed in the buyer's country, use that country's rate for the product category (standard vs reduced). Your tax engine or marketplace should map HS/tax category to a rate; a single "country default" is wrong for grocery vs electronics.

Does Amazon (or another marketplace) pay my VAT for me?

Often partially. Under marketplace facilitator / deemed supplier rules, the platform may collect VAT on certain domestic and cross-border B2C sales. You remain responsible for correct product data, B2B invoices, sales outside the marketplace, and any registrations still required.

Do I need a VAT number in every EU country?

Not necessarily. If you only ship from one EU country and qualify for OSS, a single Union OSS registration may cover B2C distance sales. If you hold stock in multiple countries, you typically need VAT registration (or a fiscal structure) in each storage country. Non-EU sellers have different entry points (IOSS, import VAT, importer of record).

What changed with the EUR 10,000 distance-selling threshold?

EU-established sellers can still use a combined EUR 10,000 annual test for eligible distance sales, but goods already located in another member state are treated separately under tightened rules effective 1 January 2025. Treat "threshold" marketing content from before 2025 as outdated.

How do B2B sales work inside the EU?

Intra-EU B2B goods often use zero-rating / reverse charge if you validate the customer's VAT ID (VIES) and meet transport rules. Services have different place-of-supply rules. You still issue compliant invoices and keep evidence.

Are digital products (ebooks, SaaS) taxed like physical goods?

No. Telecommunications, broadcasting, and electronic (TBE) services to EU consumers generally follow B2C place-of-supply rules that predate the 2021 e-commerce goods package. OSS can cover TBE, but rate and evidence rules still follow the consumer's country.

Can I reclaim import VAT on inventory I ship to an EU warehouse?

If you become VAT-registered in that country and import as a taxable person, you may recover import VAT subject to local deduction rules and evidence (customs documents, C79-equivalent). IOSS VAT is generally not the same mechanism as bulk inventory imports.

Do UK VAT rules still interact with the EU?

The UK is outside the EU VAT area. Sales to/from the EU are exports/imports with customs and UK VAT rules on the UK side, and EU rules on the EU side. Northern Ireland has special rules for goods under the Windsor Framework.
