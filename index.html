Lower rates for essentials like food, books, medicine, and children's items. Varies by member state.

What is VAT?

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax collected at each stage of the supply chain. Businesses registered for VAT charge VAT on sales (output VAT), reclaim VAT on purchases (input VAT), and remit the net difference to the tax authority.

For e-commerce sellers, the key questions are: which country's VAT applies, who must register, and which rate applies to a given product? The EU sets the framework; each member state sets its own rates and compliance details.

How to Calculate VAT-Inclusive Prices

Use the same pricing base your storefront uses (gross vs. net pricing).

Add VAT to a net price (excl. VAT):

Gross = Net × (1 + VAT% / 100)

Example: €100 net at 21% → 100 × 1.21 = €121 gross

Extract VAT from a gross price (incl. VAT):

Net = Gross / (1 + VAT% / 100)

VAT amount = Gross − Net

Example: €121 gross at 21% → net 121 / 1.21 = €100, VAT = €21

Seller FAQ

What is the difference between OSS and IOSS?

Union OSS lets you report and pay VAT on B2C sales across the EU from one registration. IOSS is only for import VAT on low-value consignments (under EUR 150) sold B2C from outside the EU.

Which VAT rate do I show at checkout?

For B2C sales taxed in the buyer's country, use that country's rate for your product category (standard vs reduced).

Does Amazon or another marketplace pay my VAT for me?

Under marketplace facilitator / deemed supplier rules, the platform may collect VAT on certain B2C sales. You remain responsible for providing correct product tax data.

Do I need a VAT number to sell on Amazon EU?

If you store goods in an EU country (e.g. via FBA), you generally need a VAT number in that country. For cross-border shipments from outside the EU, IOSS may apply for parcels under EUR 150.

What is the VAT rate for digital products in Europe?

Digital services (e-books, software, streaming) are taxed at the buyer's country standard or reduced rate. Most EU countries apply the standard rate to digital goods.

How often do I need to file VAT returns?

Filing frequency varies by country — typically monthly or quarterly. Under OSS, you file one quarterly return covering all EU countries where you have sales.
